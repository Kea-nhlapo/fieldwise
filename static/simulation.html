    Limitations

    • Problems stay until Felix visits; real infestations change and some become obvious to everyone.
    • Travel time, road access and visit length are ignored.
    • The rates are plausible assumptions, not estimates fitted to Bomet field data.
    • Fieldwise’s weights are fixed here; in a pilot they would be learned from visit outcomes.
