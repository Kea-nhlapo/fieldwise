Real public data, fictional farms. Fetch live provider responses for Tshego’s eight Kasungu scenarios. Scores are illustrative inspection priorities, not diagnoses. Survey delivery is simulated locally.

Privacy under Malawi’s Data Protection Act 2024. Weather and soil describe a place. Linking that place, its crop problems and survey answers to an identifiable farmer can create personal information. We separate environmental retrieval from farmer reports, collect only what supports a visit, and leave decisions with Felix. See the data choices ↓

Data choices, farmer control and data protection law

Environmental evidence

Public APIs receive the fictional coordinates or map extent, plus ordinary connection metadata. They receive no farmer name or survey response. Soil is a mapped estimate; weather is a forecast. Coordinates can still identify real people when linked to a farm.

Farmer control

This demo records a voluntary participation choice and notice version. Review the local record, submit a new survey to correct an answer, or delete the selected farm’s reports and notes. An unanswered survey adds no penalty.

Before a real pilot

Kasungu is in Malawi, so Malawi’s Data Protection Act 2024 applies, with MACRA as the regulator. South African deployments fall under POPIA. This prototype has not been assessed for compliance with either. Farmer answers and activity older than 180 days are deleted automatically at start-up (set FIELDWISE_RETENTION_DAYS to change it); an open concern keeps its report until Felix reviews it. There are no user log-ins or roles yet. A pilot needs a named responsible party and contact, justified lawful basis and retention period, appropriate security, operator arrangements, cross-border assessment and a route for rights requests.

Design reference: POPIA: minimality, purpose, retention, notice, safeguards, access/correction, automated decisions and transfers. Felix can inspect and override the score; it must not automatically deny support.
